I'm Torbjörn, the person behind RuToSo. In my spare time I build small Android apps and websites – mostly for myself and my family, some of them for everyone.

What I like about it is having things in my own hands: instead of using someone else's app with ads and an account for every little thing, I'd rather build something of my own, simple and without data collection.
